What We Offer

Our Products

Premium Plywood

Our flagship plywood is crafted from the finest quality timber veneers, precision-engineered for superior strength and finish. Ideal for high-end furniture, cabinetry, and interior panelling. Available in multiple grades and thicknesses, with a smooth surface ready for lamination or polish.

View Product Details →

Commercial Plywood

Built for everyday applications where cost-effectiveness meets reliable performance. Woodborn Commercial Plywood is ideal for general construction, packing, false ceilings, and partitions. Available in standard sizes with consistent thickness and strong inter-layer bonding.

View Product Details →

Waterproof Plywood (BWP/BWR)

Engineered with Boiling Water Proof (BWP) and Boiling Water Resistant (BWR) phenol formaldehyde resin for maximum moisture resistance. Perfect for kitchens, bathrooms, and outdoor applications where exposure to water or humidity is a constant challenge.

View Product Details →

Marine Plywood

The ultimate choice for extreme moisture environments. Woodborn Marine Plywood is manufactured with void-free core veneers and 100% waterproof adhesive, conforming to international marine-grade standards. Ideal for boats, docks, humid outdoor structures, and coastal construction.

View Product Details →

Block Board

Constructed with a solid timber core sandwiched between high-quality veneer sheets, Woodborn Block Board offers exceptional rigidity and screw-holding strength. The go-to choice for doors, shelving, tabletops, and long-span applications where sag resistance is critical.

View Product Details →

Flush Doors

Woodborn Flush Doors combine a solid or hollow timber core with premium face veneers for a sleek, flat finish. 100% calibrated for uniform thickness, ensuring perfect fit and long-lasting performance. Available in solid core, hollow core, and fire-rated variants for residential and commercial use.

View Product Details →

How We Make It

Technology & R&D

1

Handpicking Suitable Timber

Only the finest grade raw timber is selected for processing.

2

Precision Peeling of Core Veneer

Uniform veneer sheets are peeled with precision machinery.

3

Controlled Drying

Moisture content is reduced to optimal levels in controlled chambers.

4

Automatic Core Composing

Veneers are assembled automatically for zero-gap bonding.

5

Advanced Resin Formation

High-grade resins are applied for waterproof, long-lasting bonds.

6

Hot & Cold Press

Dual-press process ensures maximum strength and uniform thickness.
